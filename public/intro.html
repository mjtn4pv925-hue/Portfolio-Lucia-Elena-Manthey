How I think

I'm interested in the space where strategy, emotion and storytelling meet. Where actual impact is created.

5 principles

  1. Start with curiosity

    I immerse myself in people, places and ideas before looking for solutions. The best ideas begin with genuine observation.

  2. Find the human story

    Behind every system, product or challenge is a human emotion. That's where meaningful communication begins.

  3. Make complexity tangible

    I enjoy translating complex ideas into stories, experiences and visuals that people can relate to and remember (see cambiando platos).

  4. Design for belonging

    I believe the strongest ideas are the ones people can actively experience, contribute to and make their own.

  5. Stay open

    I see uncertainty as an invitation to explore, adapt and continuously evolve.
